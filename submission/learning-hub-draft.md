# TicketMaestro

TicketMaestro is a support portal for NorthLink Internet, a fictional internet provider. It uses AI to identify the support department and urgency of a request. Requests with uncertain results go to manual review.

- AI provider and model. TypeSafe Jev (`typesafe/jev`) through Cloudflare Workers AI. [Model documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/typesafe/jev/)

- Administrator access. **[Add live client URL]** and sign in as the administrator.
